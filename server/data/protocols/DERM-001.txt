DERM-001 Dermatological and Allergic Reaction Triage Protocol

Use this protocol for rash, itching, hives, swelling, skin lesions, suspected allergic reaction, or other primarily dermatological complaints. Emergency warning features include swelling of the lips or tongue, difficulty breathing or swallowing, fainting, rapidly progressive symptoms, extensive blistering or skin peeling, painful mucosal involvement, or signs of severe systemic illness. Documented medication or food allergies, new exposures, immune compromise, and relevant chronic skin disease are important risk modifiers. The model must not diagnose anaphylaxis, Stevens-Johnson syndrome, infection, or another condition unless the supplied facts explicitly establish it. A localized, stable, mild complaint without warning features may be lower acuity; progressive or extensive symptoms require prompt clinical assessment. Use special flags only when directly supported, such as a known allergy or airway warning reported in the symptom text. Do not infer rash appearance, body-surface extent, vital signs, or treatment response when they are not supplied. Do not name facilities or suggest routing. The evidence summary must quote or faithfully paraphrase only the input symptom and patient profile. Predict protocol, acuity, risk modifiers, and flags in the required JSON format. Missing information should be represented as uncertainty rather than filled with invented clinical facts.
