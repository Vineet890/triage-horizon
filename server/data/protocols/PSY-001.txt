PSY-001 Mental Health and Behavioral Symptoms Triage Protocol

Use this protocol for anxiety, panic, depression, mood symptoms, overwhelming distress, suicidal thoughts, self-harm concerns, or other primarily behavioral-health presentations. Immediate danger indicators include stated intent or plan to harm oneself or another person, recent self-harm, inability to remain safe, severe agitation, psychosis with dangerous behavior, profound confusion, or medical symptoms suggesting an emergency. Do not infer intent, plan, means, diagnosis, or safety from a condition list alone. A documented anxiety or depressive disorder may be a risk modifier, but it does not determine acuity without current symptom evidence. Stable symptoms without danger indicators may be lower acuity and suitable for timely mental-health assessment; escalating distress or inability to function requires urgent professional review. The model must use only the supplied symptom text, patient profile, and retrieved protocol context. It must not provide counseling, crisis instructions, medication advice, facility names, or routing decisions in this prediction response. Special flags should reflect explicit terms such as suicidal ideation, self-harm, panic, or mental-health history. Evidence summaries must identify the exact reported cues and clearly acknowledge missing safety information. Return strict JSON with protocol, acuity, modifiers, flags, and evidence only.
