CARD-001 Cardiac and Chest Pain Triage Protocol

Use this protocol for chest pain, pressure, tightness, palpitations, suspected acute coronary syndrome, syncope with cardiac features, or symptoms suggesting a dangerous cardiovascular process. Treat crushing or persistent chest discomfort, radiation to the arm, jaw, back, severe breathlessness, diaphoresis, new neurological symptoms, or hemodynamic instability as emergency warning features. A history of coronary disease, heart failure, diabetes, hypertension, atrial fibrillation, anticoagulant therapy, or older age increases concern but does not establish a diagnosis. The triage assessment should identify onset, duration, provoking and relieving factors, associated dyspnea, fainting, vomiting, bleeding, and known medication use. Do not recommend a facility or treatment from this protocol alone. Escalate immediately when symptoms are severe, progressive, associated with collapse, or accompanied by signs of shock. Moderate, stable symptoms require prompt specialist assessment. Mild, brief, clearly non-alarming symptoms may be suitable for clinician review, with explicit safety-net instructions. Risk modifiers should identify relevant comorbidities, medication hazards, and missing history. Evidence summaries must cite only the symptom and profile facts supplied to the model. This protocol supports acuity and flag prediction, not diagnosis, prescribing, or routing.
